# iPhone test checklist

## Stage 1: foundation

Use Safari on the iPhone 15 Pro. Tick each item, and note anything odd so it can be fixed before stage 2.

**Install**
- [ ] Open the app's GitHub Pages address in Safari.
- [ ] Share → **Add to Home Screen**. The icon is a red square with a white speech bubble and "ą"; the name is **Polski**.
- [ ] Open it from the Home Screen. It opens full screen, with no Safari address bar.

**First run and audio**
- [ ] The Welcome screen appears and says it is using a Polish voice (for example "Zosia").
- [ ] With **headphones**: tap *Test*. You hear "Dzień dobry".
- Known limits, no need to re-test: speech is muted on silent, and Enhanced/Premium voices from iPhone Settings can't be used (only the built-in "Zosia").
- [ ] Tap **Start learning**.

**Layout**
- [ ] Nothing sits under the Dynamic Island at the top or the home indicator at the bottom, on any screen.
- [ ] The main button is at the bottom of the screen and easy to reach with your thumb.
- [ ] Switch the phone to Dark Mode (Control Centre or Settings → Display). The app follows it; switch back too.

**Studying**
- [ ] Home shows Pronunciation and Grammar as "coming", and Vocabulary with 8 new cards.
- [ ] Vocabulary → **Continue**. The word *cześć* appears and is spoken automatically (after your first tap in the session).
- [ ] The speaker and *Slow* buttons replay it.
- [ ] *Show answer* shows the meaning and an example sentence; the grade buttons show times (e.g. 1m, 10m, 7d).
- [ ] Grade a few cards. Home now says fewer new cards are left today.
- [ ] Close the app fully (swipe it away) and reopen it: progress is still there.

**Settings**
- [ ] Change *New cards per day*, leave Settings, come back: the value has stuck.
- [ ] Gender switches and voice choice can be changed and stick.

**Offline**
- [ ] Turn on **Flight Mode**. Close the app fully and reopen it from the Home Screen. Everything above still works, including audio.
- [ ] Turn Flight Mode off again.

**Updates** (I'll trigger this when I deploy the next change)
- [ ] After a new version is deployed, reopening the app shows "Update available, tap to reload". Tapping it reloads into the new version, with progress kept.

The accent bar above the keyboard arrives with typed answers in stage 3 and gets its own checks then.

## Stage 2: pronunciation

Tap the **Update available** banner first (or fully close and reopen the app until it appears).

**Lessons**
- [ ] Home shows Pronunciation with new cards available. Open it: 12 topics, topic 1 highlighted as *Up next*.
- [ ] Open any topic directly (e.g. topic 7). The lesson shows, and the highlighted Polish words play when tapped. *More detail* opens and closes.
- [ ] Go back and tap **Continue**. If you haven't read the next topic's lesson, it appears first with *Start practising*.

**Exercises** (use headphones)
- [ ] **Listen and choose:** the word plays on its own (after your first tap), the options are big and easy to hit one-handed, and *Slow* replays it slowly.
- [ ] Choose a wrong option on purpose: you see the right word, its meaning and a link back to the lesson. *I was right* and *Next* are easy to reach.
- [ ] **Stress spotting** (topic 12, or topic 1): syllable buttons fit across the screen; after tapping, the stressed syllable is shown in capitals.
- [ ] **Dictation** (comes after a topic's listening cards): tap the box. The keyboard opens with the **accent bar directly on top of it** (ą ć ę ł ń ó ś ź ż and ✓).
  - [ ] Tapping a letter inserts it where the cursor is, and the keyboard stays open.
  - [ ] Nothing is autocorrected or auto-capitalised.
  - [ ] ✓ or the keyboard's Go key checks the answer.
  - [ ] Type a word without its accents: "Nearly: watch the accents", with the letters highlighted.
  - [ ] Make one typo in a longer word: "Typo? Try once more".
- [ ] The accent bar never covers the answer box, and nothing hides under the Dynamic Island or home indicator.

**Scheduling**
- [ ] Study a few cards in two different topics. The *new cards left today* number on Home goes down for both.
- [ ] Next morning (after 04:00): Home shows reviews due for Pronunciation, and they come back when you tap Continue or *Review all due*.

**Offline**
- [ ] In Flight Mode, close and reopen the app: lessons, audio and all three exercise types still work.

**Content**
- If a word, sound description or answer looks wrong, note the topic and word. (A Flag button on each card arrives in stage 5.)

## Stage 3: vocabulary

Tap the **Update available** banner first. Your 8 starter words keep their progress.

**Topics**
- [ ] Vocabulary shows 12 topics plus *My phrases* (empty until stage 5). The first topic with new words is marked *Up next*.
- [ ] Open a topic: its words are listed, and each plays when tapped.

**Exercises** (use headphones)
- [ ] **Polish → English:** the word plays on its own; *Show answer* shows the meaning, word type (e.g. "feminine noun", "imperfective; pair: zrobić"), a note where there is one, and example sentences you can play. Formal and colloquial words carry a small label.
- [ ] Core verbs (e.g. *być*, *mieć*): *Present tense* opens a small table of forms.
- [ ] **English → Polish** (only appears after you've got the Polish → English card right once): type the word with the accent bar; marking works like dictation (accents, typo retry, *I was right*).
- [ ] **Listening:** a sentence plays with no text; *Show text* reveals it and its translation; grade yourself.
- [ ] **Sentence gaps:** a sentence with a blank, its translation, and the word's dictionary form as a hint when the gap needs a different form (e.g. *kot* → *kota*).

**Gender**
- [ ] With the default settings, your own sentences say e.g. *byłem / jestem zmęczony*, and questions to your partner say e.g. *jesteś zmęczona?*. Lines your partner says are marked "(your partner says)".
- [ ] In Settings, switch *I am* to Female, then look at the same kind of sentence: it now says *byłam / zmęczona*. Switch it back.

**Keyboard**
- [ ] The accent bar sits directly on the keyboard on both typed card types, and nothing is autocorrected or capitalised.

**Content**
- If a word, translation or sentence looks wrong or unnatural, note the topic and the Polish. (Flag buttons arrive in stage 5.)

## Stage 4: grammar

Tap the **Update available** banner first. (If a module says *All done for now*, today's 15 new cards are used up; raise *New cards per day* in Settings while testing.)

**Explanations**
- [ ] Grammar shows 8 topics; open one directly. The explanation is one or two short paragraphs, a small table where it helps (readable at phone width; it scrolls sideways if needed), and *More detail* collapsed.
- [ ] Highlighted Polish words in the explanation and table play when tapped.
- [ ] **Continue** shows a topic's explanation before its first drill.

**Drills**
- [ ] Early drills in a topic are **choose the form**: the sentence has a blank, the base form in brackets (e.g. *Idę do ? (sklep)*), the English underneath, and big option buttons at the bottom.
- [ ] Later drills are **typed**, with the accent bar on the keyboard.
- [ ] Get one wrong on purpose: you see the right form in the full sentence, a one-line reason, and a link back to the explanation. The link opens the topic.
- [ ] *I was right* and *Next* are easy to reach.

**Gender** (past tense, topic 8)
- [ ] With default settings, your own sentences use *byłem / robiłem* and questions to your partner use *byłaś / robiłaś*.
- [ ] Switch *I am* to Female in Settings: the same drill now expects *byłam*. Switch back.

**Offline**
- [ ] In Flight Mode, grammar explanations and drills still work.

## Stage 5: finishing

Tap the **Update available** banner first.

**Stats**
- [ ] Home → *Stats*: days studied, cards learned, cards due in the next 7 days (with a small bar chart, readable in light and dark mode) and accuracy per module. No streaks.

**My phrases**
- [ ] Home → *Add phrase*: type Polish (accent bar appears), English and an optional note; *Hear it* speaks the Polish. *Add phrase* saves it.
- [ ] Vocabulary → *My phrases* lists it with *Edit*; *Study* shows it as a Polish → English card with audio.
- [ ] Edit it (change the English) and save; delete another one (it asks first).

**Flags**
- [ ] On any card, tap *⚑ Flag* (top right of the card), add a note, *Save flag*. The button now says *Flagged*.
- [ ] Settings → *Flagged cards* lists it; *Copy* and *Export as text* both work; *Remove* takes it off the list.

**Session timer**
- [ ] A thin bar appears at the very top (under the Dynamic Island, not hidden by it) once you start studying, and slowly fills.
- [ ] Settings → *Session timer*: try 20 minutes, or Off (the bar disappears). When the time is up, a small message says so and you can carry on.

**Backup** (the important one)
- [ ] Settings → *Export progress*: iOS offers to save the file; save it to Files.
- [ ] *Import from file…* → pick that file: it shows a summary (cards, reviews, phrases, flags) and asks before replacing. After it reloads, Home shows the same due counts and new-card allowance as before, and Stats shows the same numbers.

**Reset**
- [ ] *Reset all progress* asks twice; tapping Cancel on either keeps everything. (Only go through with it after exporting, if at all.)

**Update banner**
- [ ] (Next time I deploy a change) reopening the app shows *Update available, tap to reload*, and tapping it keeps all progress.
